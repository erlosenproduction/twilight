About Us

Crafting Coffee with Passion & Purpose

Founded in 2018, AURA was born out of a desire to redefine modern cafe culture. We source directly from sustainable farms across Central America and East Africa, micro-roasting in small batches to preserve every subtle tasting note.

Whether you are searching for a quiet morning espresso, a cozy workspace, or artisanal breakfast items, AURA is your sanctuary in the heart of the city.
